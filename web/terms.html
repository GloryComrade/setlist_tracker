Terms and Crew rules

The deal for using Setlist Tracker, and the rules for The Crew. Joining a Crew means you agree to them.

Effective November 22, 2026

Back to the front page

The short version

  • Be decent to your Crew. Zero tolerance for abuse.
  • Report or block anyone, any time, in one tap.
  • Reports are read within 24 hours. Rule breakers lose their account.
  • The plans are general training guidance, not medical advice.

Crew rules

The Crew is for running friends. Whatever your Crew can see, from your stage name and profile photo to your Show notes and Tour, has to follow these rules. Don’t post:

  • harassment, bullying, threats or anything that targets someone for who they are
  • hate speech, or praise for violence or hate groups
  • nudity or sexual content
  • someone else’s private information, or a photo of someone without their OK
  • spam, ads, scams or anything illegal

There is zero tolerance for this. Content that breaks the rules is removed, and so is the account that posted it.

Reporting and blocking

On any Show in The Crew, tap the three-dot menu, then Report this Show or Block. To report a profile photo, tap the friend, then Report photo. A Show you report disappears from your Crew feed right away, and blocking someone removes them from your Crew in both directions.

Every report reaches the developer, who reviews it within 24 hours and removes content and accounts that break the rules. You can also email ryanchengpictures@gmail.com.

Nobody can find you unless you invite them, so blocking is final.

Training advice

Setlist Tracker gives general training guidance, not medical advice. Tours are built from simple rules, not from your health history. If you’re injured, ill or unsure, check with a doctor or coach before you run, and stop if something hurts.

Your account and your data

You own what you log. You let Setlist Tracker store it and show it to your Crew so the app can work, as described in the Privacy policy. Delete account erases it. Setlist Tracker is not meant for children under 13.

The app is free and provided as is. It may change, and features may come and go. On iPhone, Apple’s standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement also applies.

Changes and questions

If these terms change, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Email ryanchengpictures@gmail.com.
