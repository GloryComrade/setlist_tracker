Privacy

Your training is yours. This is everything Setlist Tracker keeps, where it lives, who else touches it, and how to delete it. Short enough to read before your next easy run.

Effective November 22, 2026

Back to the front page

The short version

  • No account? Your log stays on your device.
  • Backstage Pass? It’s stored on Cloudflare so your devices can sync.
  • Your Crew sees the Shows you log, unless you mark one Keep off the Crew.
  • Only you see who’s seen your Shows, and you can stop being counted.
  • No ads, no analytics, nothing sold.
  • Delete account erases it all, right away.

The long version is below, and it’s the one that counts.

Without an account

Everything you log (Shows, Tours, Gear, Saved Mixes, songs) stays on your device. None of it is sent to me. The one thing that leaves your device is a song search, if you make one; see Song search below.

The catch: on the website your log lives in the browser, so clearing its site data erases it.

With a Backstage Pass

A Backstage Pass is the app’s word for signing in. When you sign in with Google or Apple, I keep:

  • Your account Your name and email from Google or Apple, and an id for that sign-in. If you use Hide My Email with Apple, I only see Apple’s relay address.
  • Your training The Shows, Tours, Gear and Saved Mixes you log, so they sync between your devices.
  • The Crew, only if you use it Your stage name, avatar color and profile picture (a drawn badge, or a photo if you upload one), the people in your Crew, Encores, Shout-outs, which of your Crew’s Shows you’ve seen, invite codes, blocks, and reports you send.

The people in your Crew see your training: every Show you log (date, distance, time, pace, effort, feel, notes, Tracks, route name and GPS route, weather, Gear and Song of the Show), your Gold Records and your current Tour. Mark a Show “Keep off the Crew” and it stays yours. If you were in a Crew before November 22, 2026, your Crew keeps seeing only the Shows you shared until you say OK to this in the app. After that, Shows you logged before that moment stay hidden unless you had shared them. Your profile photo, if you upload one, is shown only to you and your Crew; you can remove it in Settings at any time, and Delete account erases it. Leave or block someone and they stop seeing it. Nobody outside your Crew can find you or see your training.

Encores, Seen by and Shout-outs. When you give a Show an Encore, the Show’s owner can see it was you, and so can anyone in your own Crew who can see that Show. When a Crew mate’s Show is on your screen for a second or so, or you open it, I note that you saw it and when. That’s “Seen by”, and only the Show’s owner sees it, on their own Shows. Nobody else, not even the rest of your Crew, sees what you’ve looked at. Don’t want it? Turn off Settings, then The Crew, then Let my Crew know when I’ve seen their Shows. From then on your views aren’t recorded, and the ones already kept are deleted. A Shout-out is a short comment (up to 280 characters) on a Show. The Show’s owner and their Crew see it. You can delete your own Shout-outs, and the Show’s owner can delete any on their Show. Anyone who can see a Shout-out can report it; the reported text is kept with the report so it can be reviewed. Delete account erases your views and your Shout-outs along with everything else.

No search, no public profile. A Crew is invite only.

Apple Health

On iPhone you can import runs from Apple Health: the app reads running workouts only when you tap Import, and they become Shows like any other. The Shows you book are saved to Health as running workouts (with the route, when you went Live), and you can turn that off in Settings. A Show’s page reads your heart rate from Health for the time of that run, if your Apple Watch caught it. Heart rate stays on your iPhone: it is never stored by the app, never sent to my server and never shown to your Crew. Health data is never used for advertising or sold.

Location (Go Live)

On iPhone, Go Live records a run with GPS. The app uses your location only while you are On Stage, from the moment you tap Go Live until you end the Show, including with the screen locked (iPhone shows the blue location pill while it does). The route is saved on the Show. If you have a Backstage Pass it syncs with your Shows, and your Crew sees it, like every other part of a Show, unless you mark that Show “Keep off the Crew”. Delete the Show and the route goes with it. Location is never used at any other time, never tracked in the background between runs, and never used for advertising or sold.

Notifications

On iPhone, the app can tell you when someone in your Crew logs a Show, and when someone gives a Shout-out on one of your Shows. It asks first, the first time you open The Crew, and you can turn each one off in Settings, then The Crew (or turn them all off in iOS Settings). To send them, I keep the push token iOS gives the app for your phone, linked to your account. Apple delivers them, and each one carries only the Crew mate’s stage name and either the Show’s workout and miles or the Shout-out’s text. Sign out on that phone and its token is deleted. Delete account deletes it too.

Song search

When you search for a Song of the Show, the words you type go to Spotify (through my server) and to Apple’s music catalog to find matches. No account details go with them.

Who handles the data

Cloudflare
hosts the website and stores the data above.
Google and Apple
handle sign-in.
Apple
delivers notifications on iPhone.
Spotify and Apple
answer song searches.

There are no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Backstage, then Backstage Pass, then the three-dot menu, then Delete account. This deletes your account and everything listed above, on every device, right away, and signs you out of Sign in with Apple for this app.

Signing out instead keeps your data in your account and clears it from that device.

Sign out is “see you next Tour.” Delete account is the last show.

Children

Setlist Tracker is not meant for children under 13.
