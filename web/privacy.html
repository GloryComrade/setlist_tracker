PRIVACY

Your training is yours. Here is everything Setlist Tracker keeps, where it lives, and how to delete it.

Effective November 22, 2026

Without an account

Everything you log (Shows, Tours, Gear, Saved Mixes, songs) stays on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

With a Backstage Pass

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we keep:

  • Your account: your name and email from Google or Apple, and an id for that sign-in. If you use Hide My Email with Apple, we only see Apple’s relay address.
  • Your training: the Shows, Tours, Gear and Saved Mixes you log, so they sync between your devices.
  • The Crew, only if you use it: your stage name and avatar color, the people in your Crew, the Shows you choose to share (date, distance, pace, time, Song of the Show, Gold Records, and notes only if you tick them), Encores, invite codes, blocks, and reports you send.

Your Crew sees only what you share with them. Nobody else can find you or see your training.

Apple Health

On iPhone you can import runs from Apple Health. The app only reads running workouts, only when you tap Import, and never writes to Health. Imported runs become Shows like any other. Health data is never used for advertising or sold.

Song search

When you search for a Song of the Show, the words you type go to Spotify (through our server) and to Apple’s music catalog to find matches. No account details go with them.

Who handles the data

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and stores the data above.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in.
  • Spotify and Apple answer song searches.

There are no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Backstage → Backstage Pass → ⋮ → Delete account. This deletes your account and everything listed above, on every device, right away, and signs you out of Sign in with Apple for this app. Signing out instead keeps your data in your account and clears it from that device.

Children

Setlist Tracker is not meant for children under 13.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? See Support.
