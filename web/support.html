Common questions

How do I sync between my iPhone and the website? Sign in with the same Google or Apple account on both, under Backstage → Backstage Pass.
How do I add a friend to my Crew? Open The Crew, tap Invite a friend, and send them the link or code. Codes last 7 days.
How do I stop sharing a Show? Open the Show and turn off Share with your Crew. It leaves your Crew’s feed right away.
How do I block or report someone? On their Show, tap ⋯ and choose Report or Block. You can also open them from Your Crew and tap Block.
How do I delete my account? Backstage → Backstage Pass → ⋮ → Delete account. Everything is deleted on every device. See Privacy.

Training advice
