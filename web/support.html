Common questions

Short answers, in the app’s own words. If yours isn’t here, the GitHub link above is the way in.

Your Backstage Pass

How do I sync between my iPhone and the website?

Sign in with the same Google or Apple account on both, under Backstage, then Backstage Pass. The Shows already on each device come along, and from then on they stay in step.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Backstage, then Backstage Pass, then the three-dot menu, then Delete account. Everything is deleted on every device. Privacy has the details.

The Crew

How do I add a friend to my Crew?

Open The Crew, tap Invite a friend, and send them the link or the code. Codes last 7 days, so if a friend sits on it past next weekend’s Headliner, send a new one.

How do I keep a Show to myself?

Open the Show and turn on Keep off the Crew. Only you see it after that.

How do I block or report someone?

On one of their shared Shows, tap the three-dot menu and choose Report this Show or Block. You can also open them from Your Crew and tap Block.
